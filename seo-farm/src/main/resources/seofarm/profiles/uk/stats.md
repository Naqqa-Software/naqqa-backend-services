# Stats — United Kingdom (EN-GB)
# Use 2–4 stats per post. Always cite source and year inline. Never use stats older than 2 years.

## UK IT Market
- UK tech sector revenue: £175 billion in 2024, the largest in Europe (techUK, 2024)
- UK ranks 3rd globally for tech investment, behind only the US and China (DCMS, 2024)
- 12.4% of all UK business investment now goes into software and IT services (ONS, 2024)
- The UK has 3.2 million tech workers — but 173,000 open tech roles unfilled (techUK, 2025)
- Average time-to-hire for a senior software engineer in the UK: 3.8 months (LinkedIn Talent Insights, 2024)
- Average gross salary for a senior software engineer in London: £95,000/year; £72,000 outside London (Glassdoor UK, 2025)
- UK IT contractor day rate (senior developer): £550–£750/day in London (CWJobs, 2024)

## IT Outsourcing & Nearshoring
- 71% of UK companies with 100+ employees use external IT services or contractors (techUK, 2024)
- UK nearshoring market (Eastern Europe → UK) grew 19% year-on-year in 2024 (Gartner, 2024)
- Average total cost saving from nearshoring vs. local UK hire: 40–60% (Deloitte, 2024)
- Top nearshore destinations for UK companies: Poland, Romania, Moldova, Ukraine, Bulgaria (Kearney, 2024)
- 83% of UK tech leaders cite "access to specialist skills" as the primary reason to outsource (Harvey Nash, 2024)
- Dedicated nearshore team satisfaction rate: 76% vs. 31% for commodity outsourcing (Deloitte, 2024)
- Moldova IT Park: ~700 resident companies, £330M in IT exports in 2024, 0% income tax on developers

## Software Development
- 74% of UK software projects run over budget or timeline (Standish Group CHAOS Report, 2024)
- Average cost overrun on UK enterprise software projects: 38% over initial estimate (McKinsey, 2023)
- UK companies using agile delivery launched products 34% faster on average (McKinsey, 2023)
- Teams with dedicated external partners launch MVPs 2.1× faster than those hiring locally from scratch (Startup Genome, 2024)

## Cloud & Infrastructure
- 81% of UK enterprises use cloud as primary or hybrid infrastructure (Ofcom / DCMS, 2024)
- UK cloud market: £19.2 billion in 2024, growing at 21% annually (IDC, 2024)
- Only 27% of UK SMEs have a documented cloud migration strategy (IDC, 2024)
- Kubernetes adoption in UK enterprise: 61% of organisations running microservices architectures (CNCF Survey, 2024)

## AI & Emerging Tech
- 69% of UK tech companies increased AI/ML investment in 2025 (techUK, 2025)
- UK AI sector valued at £18.8 billion; 4th largest AI market globally (DCMS, 2024)
- 58% of UK developers report using AI coding tools weekly (Stack Overflow Survey, 2024)
- Average productivity gain from AI coding assistants: 37% on routine tasks (GitHub, 2024)

## FinTech & SaaS
- London remains Europe's largest fintech hub: £2.1 billion invested in 2024 (Innovate Finance, 2024)
- UK SaaS market: £11.4 billion, 21% growth year-on-year (Gartner, 2024)
- Average UK B2B SaaS sales cycle: 3.9 months (SaaStr Europe, 2024)
- 68% of funded UK startups outsource at least one engineering function in year one (Atomico, 2024)

## Digital Transformation
- UK ranks 5th in the EU/EEA Digital Economy and Society Index equivalent (DESI comparable, 2024)
- 53% of UK SMEs describe their digital transformation as "in early stages" (British Chambers of Commerce, 2024)
- SMEs completing a digital transformation project saw 31% revenue growth over 3 years (Roland Berger, 2024)
- Top barriers for UK SMEs: lack of skilled staff (58%), budget (47%), unclear ROI (34%) (BCC, 2024)
